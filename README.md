Superstore Profit Decline Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes the Superstore dataset to investigate factors contributing to profit performance across different regions, categories, sub-categories, and customer segments.

The analysis focuses on identifying underperforming areas and understanding how sales, profit, discounts, and sales volume affect overall business performance.

Business Problem

The business experienced declining or inconsistent profit performance across some regions.

The objective of this analysis is to identify the major factors contributing to the decline and provide data-driven recommendations that can support better business decisions.

Objectives

- Analyze sales and profit performance across regions.
- Identify underperforming categories and sub-categories.
- Examine sales and profit trends.
- Investigate the effect of discounts on profitability.
- Compare regional performance.
- Identify areas that require management attention.
- Provide recommendations for improving profitability.

Analysis

The analysis examined:

Regional Performance

Sales and profit were compared across the Central, East, South, and West regions to identify differences in performance.

Category and Sub-category Performance

Product categories and sub-categories were analyzed to identify products generating strong sales and those contributing less to profitability.

Discount Analysis

Discount levels were examined to understand their relationship with profit. High discounts can increase sales volume while reducing profit margins.

Sales and Profit Trends

Sales and profit trends were reviewed to identify periods of strong and weak performance.

Key Insights

The analysis highlights differences in profitability across regions, categories, and sub-categories.

Some products generate high sales but relatively low profit, demonstrating that high sales do not always translate into high profitability.

Discounting also has an important impact on profit, particularly where discounts are high.

Recommendations

- Review products and sub-categories with consistently low or negative profit.
- Reduce excessive discounting where it does not generate sufficient additional sales.
- Focus on products and regions with strong profitability.
- Monitor profit margins alongside sales when evaluating business performance.
- Develop targeted strategies for underperforming regions and product categories.

Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides interactive visualizations for exploring sales, profit, regional performance, product performance, and other business metrics.
